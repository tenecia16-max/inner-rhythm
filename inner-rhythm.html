The emotional side
This part does not get talked about enough
Trying to conceive can mess with your emotions in ways nobody really warns you about. One moment you are hopeful, the next anxious, and sometimes you feel nothing at all — which is also completely fine. Your cycle still affects all of this. The luteal phase especially can feel brutal when you are in the two-week wait. I will keep checking in on how you are doing, not just where you are in your cycle.
Gentle reminders
Timing
The 5 to 6 days leading up to and including ovulation are when conception is most possible. You do not need to be precise to the hour — being in that window is what matters.
Your body
Some people notice signs around ovulation — a slight change in discharge, a dull ache on one side, or just feeling a bit different. You might start recognizing your own signals the more you check in.
The two-week wait
After your fertile window closes, there is nothing to do but wait — and that is genuinely hard. Your luteal phase symptoms can mimic early pregnancy signs too, which makes it even more confusing. Whatever you are feeling in that window, it makes sense.
A note on pressure
If this starts feeling like a job, that is worth noticing. Stress genuinely affects cycles. If you need to take a month off from tracking and just live, that is a completely valid choice — and not giving up.

What Inner Rhythm is
Inner Rhythm helps you understand how your cycle affects your mood, energy, communication and relationships — not just your body.

The more you check in, the more the app learns your specific patterns and tailors its messages to you personally. After a few cycles, it stops sounding like a generic wellness app and starts sounding like it actually knows you.

It uses AI to write personalised daily messages based on your recent logs. Your data never leaves your phone — the AI only receives anonymised context, not your raw entries.
Privacy policy
Your data stays on your device. Inner Rhythm stores all your cycle data, check-ins, notes and profile information in your phone's local storage only. We do not have a server that holds your personal health data. We cannot see it, access it or retrieve it.
AI personalisation. When you have 3 or more check-ins, the app sends a short summary of your recent patterns (phase, cycle day, recent moods and feelings — never your notes or personal details) to Claude AI by Anthropic to generate a personalised message. This context is not stored by Inner Rhythm after the message is generated.
Partner sharing. The companion link you can share with a partner encodes your current phase information into the URL itself. It is generated on your device and we never see or store it. Your partner only sees what you choose to share.
Feedback. If you submit feedback through the app, your responses are sent to Formspree and emailed to the Inner Rhythm team. This is the only data that leaves your device intentionally, and only if you choose to submit.
We do not: sell your data, use advertising trackers, share your information with third parties, store your health data on any server, or use your data to train AI models.
Terms of use
Inner Rhythm is provided as a beta product for informational and self-awareness purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
Do not use Inner Rhythm as a contraceptive method. Cycle tracking is not a reliable form of birth control.
If you are experiencing severe symptoms — physical or mental — please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
By using Inner Rhythm, you agree that you understand and accept these terms. Inner Rhythm reserves the right to update these terms at any time.
